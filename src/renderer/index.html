Welcome to whim

A few things that make whim fast. Reopen this anytime with the ? in the header.

  • ⌨️
    Summon whim from anywhere
    Press to pop whim open, capture a thought, and dismiss.
  • 🖱️
    Or use the tray icon
    whim always sits in the menu bar / system tray — click its icon any time to bring the pane back.
  • 🎙️
    Capture by voice
    With the box empty, press Space to record. Transcription runs locally on your machine.
  • 🤖
    Put agents to work
    Open a canvas, select text, and @mention a persona to launch an agent on it.
  • 📄
    Open a canvas
    Click a space (or press Enter) to open its rich markdown canvas.

⚙ Settings

General

Appearance
System follows your operating system's light/dark setting.
Window Behavior
When unchecked, the side pane stays visible like a normal window. Pinning makes all windows stay on top.
Editor
When unchecked (default), comments only open when your cursor moves into a commented selection. Commented links and resource pills still respond to hover.
Export Destinations
Push a copy of a canvas (PDF, Word, or Markdown) into a folder for quick access and sharing. Point these at folders synced by OneDrive/SharePoint or Google Drive desktop apps and your exports show up in the cloud — and on your phone — automatically.
Updates
whim
When on, updates download in the background and prompt you to restart. When off, you're notified and can download manually. Updates install on the next restart either way.
Workspace
Not set
The working directory for Copilot CLI sessions.
Profiles
Keep separate repos (e.g. work and personal) and switch between them from the whim logo at the bottom of the panel or with the Switch Profile hotkey. Each profile can carry its own color tint.
