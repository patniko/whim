Quick start

whim is a side pane that hides until you need it. Two things to learn first — it takes about 20 seconds.

Step 1 of 2

Summon and dismiss whim

One shortcut slides whim in from the edge of your screen — and slides it away again. It works from any app.

Prefer a different combo? Click the shortcut above and press the keys you want.
  1. Press it once — whim slides away
  2. Press it again — whim comes back
Step 2 of 2

whim also lives in your menu bar

Forget the shortcut? Click the whim icon in the menu bar and the pane comes right back.

Welcome to whim

A few things that make whim fast. Reopen this anytime with the ? in the header.

  • ⌨️
    Summon whim from anywhere
    Press to pop whim open, capture a thought, and dismiss.
  • 🖱️
    Or use the tray icon
    whim always sits in the menu bar / system tray — click its icon any time to bring the pane back.
  • 🎙️
    Capture by voice
    With the box empty, press Space to record. Transcription runs locally on your machine.
  • 🤖
    Put agents to work
    Open a canvas, select text, and @mention a persona to launch an agent on it.
  • 📄
    Open a canvas
    Click a space (or press Enter) to open its rich markdown canvas.
